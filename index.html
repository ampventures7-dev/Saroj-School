HERITAGE OF EMINENCE

About Saroj Mehta International School, Dapoli

Established in 2016 under the visionary aegis of Santoshbhai Mehta Foundation, Dapoli, Saroj Mehta International School (SMISK) is widely recognized as one of the most distinguished CBSE day schools in the Ratnagiri district of Maharashtra.

Nestled in the lush, serene environs of Karanjani, Dapoli, SMISK seamlessly blends academic rigor with character education, sportsmanship, and environmental stewardship. Our modern pedagogy empowers every child to cultivate intellectual curiosity, emotional intelligence, and global awareness.
